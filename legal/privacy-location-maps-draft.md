## Location

Jaderoo uses approximate location by default. A precise GPS fix is used only after you tap "Use my exact location" and confirm that you want it.

You can pick a spot on the map instead. A spot you pick is handled like an approximate location. It stays on this device. It is not saved on our servers as a home.

We use your approximate location, or a spot you pick, to show activities within the radius you choose. We don't store your exact location on our servers.

Requests that need a location are rounded to about a kilometer before they leave the device. Shared links do not include where you are. We don't put your coordinates in server storage, logs, or analytics.

## The map

The map loads tiles directly from the U.S. Geological Survey's National Map. That service receives your IP address and the map area you view. Jaderoo's servers do not receive or store those tiles.

If the map tiles don't load, you can still search for a place and set the radius.

## What stays on this device

Your location, a spot you pick, and the radius you choose are stored in local storage on your device.

Clear location removes a GPS fix and a picked spot from this device. Reset app data, in your profile, removes the rest of what this device has saved for Jaderoo.
